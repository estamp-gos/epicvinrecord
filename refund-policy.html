Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

At EpicVINRecord, our goal is to provide customers with accurate and useful vehicle history information in a fast and convenient manner. If you experience an issue with your order or report, please contact our support team so we can review the matter and assist you.

1. Digital Service

EpicVINRecord provides vehicle history reports as a digital service. Reports are generated based on the vehicle information submitted by the customer and are delivered electronically.

Because reports are digital products and are generally generated immediately after purchase, cancellation or refund requests may be subject to the circumstances of the individual order.

2. If There Is an Issue With Your Report

If you believe that:

  • Your report was not delivered;
  • Your report contains an issue;
  • You entered incorrect vehicle information;
  • You received a report that does not correspond to the information you submitted; or
  • You have another concern regarding your purchase,

please contact our support team as soon as possible.

We will review your order and determine the appropriate resolution. Depending on the circumstances, we may provide assistance, a corrected report where possible, or a refund.

3. Incorrect Vehicle Information

Customers are responsible for reviewing the vehicle information they enter before completing payment.

If incorrect information was submitted during checkout, please contact us immediately. We will review the order and determine whether the report can be corrected or whether another resolution is available.

4. Refund Requests

To request a refund, please contact our support team through the refund request form below, or through the official contact method provided on our website.

Please include:

  • Order ID
  • Email address used for the purchase
  • Vehicle registration/VIN submitted with the order
  • Reason for the refund request

Providing this information allows us to locate and review your order more quickly.

5. Contact Us Before Escalating a Payment Issue

If you have a problem with your purchase, please contact EpicVINRecord support first. We are happy to review the order and work with you to resolve the issue.

Our support team can investigate delivery issues, report concerns, and eligible refund requests directly.

6. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will normally be returned through the original payment method. The time required for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your payment provider or financial institution.

7. Questions

If you are unsure whether your order qualifies for a refund, please contact us. We encourage customers to contact our support team before taking further action so that we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue.
